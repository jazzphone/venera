Venera @VERSION@

Graphics and video for macOS virtual machines on Proxmox: the desktop and apps draw with Metal on the host's GPU (virtio-gpu with Venus), and video can decode and encode on the host's GPU too.

This installer:

  • adds Venera's core kext to OpenCore and sets vgpuaccel=4 in its boot arguments (config.plist is backed up first, next to it);
  • puts two kexts in /Library/Extensions and rebuilds the Auxiliary Kernel Collection;
  • installs the Metal driver and a watcher that keeps it in place across macOS updates;
  • with the choices under Customize: hardware video, the Venera app, and Safari WebGL.

Before you start: the VM uses vga: virtio-gl on the host, and OpenCore allows unsigned kexts (SIP 0x203) and amfi_get_out_of_my_way=1. If it doesn't, the installer sets them in OpenCore, and they take effect at the restart.

A restart finishes it. If the desktop doesn't come back on Metal, Venera falls back to the software desktop by itself.

Hardware video also needs the host's half: run install-host.sh on the Proxmox host (Venera-host-@VERSION@.tar.gz). To remove Venera: sudo "/Library/Application Support/Venera/uninstall.sh".
