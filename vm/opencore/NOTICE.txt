Venera's OpenCore image (OpenCore-Venera-VERSION.img), made by vm/opencore/build.sh in Venera's repository:

- OpenCore (BOOTx64.efi, OpenCore.efi, OpenRuntime.efi, OpenHfsPlus.efi, OpenPartitionDxe.efi, ResetNvramEntry.efi)
  from acidanthera's OpenCorePkg release of that version, unmodified. BSD-3-Clause (licenses/OpenCorePkg-BSD-3.txt);
  https://github.com/acidanthera/OpenCorePkg
- config.plist: OpenCorePkg's Docs/Sample.plist with Venera's settings (vm/opencore/config.py)
- ACPI/SSDT-VENERA.aml: Venera's own (vm/opencore/SSDT-VENERA.dsl)
- Kexts/VeneraHide.kext: Venera's own (kext/VeneraHide), under Venera's license
- Kexts/Lilu.kext and Kexts/VirtualSMC.kext: from acidanthera's Lilu and VirtualSMC releases of the versions in
  packaging/UPSTREAM, unmodified. BSD-3-Clause (licenses/Lilu-BSD-3.txt, licenses/VirtualSMC-BSD-3.txt);
  https://github.com/acidanthera/Lilu, https://github.com/acidanthera/VirtualSMC

The VM script also carries macrecovery.py from OpenCorePkg (BSD-3-Clause), unmodified.
